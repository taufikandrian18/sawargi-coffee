# Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]

Sawargi Coffee ("Sawargi", "we") sells Ciwidey Natural coffee at
website.taufikandrian.my.id/sawargi-coffee. This page explains what personal data we collect, why,
where it is kept and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who is responsible: [YOUR FULL NAME / BUSINESS NAME], [CITY], Indonesia.
Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect

**When you browse the site.** The site has no analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not set
cookies on the main site. Our server keeps standard access logs (IP address, page requested, browser
type, time) to keep the site running and secure. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your
browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address
([Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

**When you order.** Checkout runs on our WooCommerce shop. We collect your name, email address, phone
number, shipping address, the coffee you order and any note you add. We use these to process,
ship and support your order, and to send you order emails. The shop sets cookies needed for the
cart and checkout to work (for example `woocommerce_cart_hash` and `wp_woocommerce_session_*`).
They are not used for tracking.

**Payment.** You pay by bank transfer to our account. We see the transfer in our bank statement
(sender name, amount, reference). We never receive or store card details or your banking
login.

**When you contact us.** If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you.

## Why we use it (legal basis)

- To fulfil your order: the contract between us.
- To keep records we're required to keep, such as sales records for tax.
- To keep the site secure and working: our legitimate interest.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for marketing without asking you first.

## Who we share it with

- **The courier** delivering your order: your name, phone number and address.
- **Our hosting provider**, [Tencent Cloud, REGION], whose servers store the site and shop.
- **Google Drive**, where encrypted backups of the shop database are kept (see below).
- Authorities, if the law requires it.

## Backups

Every night we back up the shop database, which includes orders, to our own server. We also keep an
encrypted copy in a Google Drive account we control. The backup tool only creates, reads and deletes
these backup files. It does not read or share any other Google Drive data. Backups are kept for up
to 60 days and then deleted.

## How long we keep it

Order records: [e.g. 10 YEARS, CHECK YOUR TAX-RECORD OBLIGATION]. Server access logs: [e.g. 30 DAYS].
Emails: as long as needed to answer you. After that we delete or anonymise the data. Old backups are
removed within 60 days.

## Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 Tahun 2022) you can ask to see the data we
hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent you gave. We may keep records the law
requires us to keep. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]; we reply within [14] days.

## Security

The site and shop are served over HTTPS. The admin area is password-protected, and the server is
firewalled. Off-server backups are encrypted before they leave our server.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
