# Scholar Evaluation: Evidence base for "Dried in the Fruit" (/journal/ciwidey-natural)

Artifact: narrative literature synthesis written for a brand journal. It cites 12 sources.
Scope: targeted. The review covers literature/context, data and evidence, results and
interpretation, limitations, and citations. The article has no methodology of its own, so
the methodology dimension doesn't apply.

## Overall Assessment

- Overall score: 3.5 / 5
- Confidence: medium
- Summary: The processing chemistry is well supported. The sugar and germination argument
  rests on primary research (Knopp et al. 2006; Selmar et al. 2006), and the microbiology
  rests on recent reviews. The Ciwidey-specific claims are weak by comparison: one Slow Food
  record from 2018 and one seller listing. The article says this openly in its "Where the
  evidence runs out" section. We found no peer-reviewed study of natural-processed Ciwidey
  coffee.

## Critical Issues

1. **No Ciwidey-specific natural-process data.** Every processing claim is extrapolated from
   other origins. The article discloses this. Do not add origin-specific flavour or safety
   claims without local data.
2. **Regional figures are dated.** The Slow Food entry was last modified in 2018. The
   hectare figures and the "mostly washed" claim may no longer hold.

## Recommended Revisions

- Replace [2] with a peer-reviewed history source on the Preangerstelsel, e.g. the UPI
  *Historia* journal article "Coffee Priangan in the Nineteenth Century".
- Read the full text of [11] and [12] and confirm the 12 vs 7 day and ≤12% moisture figures.
- Complete citation metadata that couldn't be verified this session (a web-fetch rate limit
  blocked it): authors and journal for [9]–[12], volume and issue for [5].
- Add Sawargi's own data: drying days, final moisture, and cupping scores per batch.
  First-party measurement is the strongest evidence this article could carry.

## Evidence Checks Needed

- Direct check of sucrose invariance in Knopp et al. 2006 (currently via [6]).
- Current Ciwidey processing mix: interviews or data from local cooperatives or Dinas
  Perkebunan Jawa Barat.

  The GI isn't mentioned now because it wasn't verified from a primary source.
